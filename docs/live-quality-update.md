# Simplified live matches and gameplay quality

Regular play still takes six minutes. Possessions now use a clock-derived twelve-second cycle with 1.5-second actions, including scaled legacy saves. Player bodies no longer reset when an event changes. Passes connect actual rendered player positions; shots originate at the shooter and goals are revealed on ball arrival. Kickoff keeps each formation in its own half, the conceding side restarts, and halftime switches longitudinal and lateral orientation. Minor stoppages no longer build defensive player blocks.

One Wechseln button opens squad-style formation and bench cards. Drag/drop, touch dragging, tap selection and keyboard activation stage a replacement; confirmation checks current position, eligibility, remaining substitutions and no re-entry. Cancellation has no effects. The panel retains the existing continue-play policy. Fitness remains in mechanics and Best XI but is not shown in the live interface.

New goal events store explicit assists, while legacy contributions are retained as a fallback. Penalties and rebounds do not fabricate assists. Live scoreboards, summaries and new archived labels use shared goal data. Opponent scorers are chosen from the saved game XI. Recorded saves, shots, combinations and cards contribute bounded ±0.4 rating adjustments with disclosed breakdowns. Existing source technical attributes are reused, with original overall ratings as fallbacks; technical changes to expected goals are capped at ±0.15. Sequential action seeds use avalanche mixing to prevent runs of identical scenes.

Travel adds roadworks, missed exits, playlist/headphone incidents, crowded trains, platform/connection changes, airport security/gates/baggage and stadium parking/fans/entrances. Actual route segments and journey phase constrain selection; eight recent event templates reduce repetition. No travel form bonuses, timing changes, new currencies, changed pack odds or prices were introduced. Duplicate rewards, saved balances, transfers, home/away policy, ten-match archives and aggregate careers remain intact. Multiplayer protocol 9 requires older clients to reload without changing their worlds.

Verification includes typecheck, production build, artifact validation, all existing tests and eight new regression tests. A controlled 900-match sample against rating 75 produced 69/300 wins at rating 65, 131/300 at 75 and 190/300 at 85, retaining underdog wins. Desktop and 390px browser views were inspected with isolated synthetic saves. Tested live pitch/score/assists, valid and invalid substitutions, cancellation, tactics, statistics, non-blocking notifications, post-match pitch removal, ten reports, career sort/filter, pack and club navigation, and a persisted away journey/map. Visual findings fixed collapsed formation width, overlapping mobile cards, inherited grid rules and card-control contrast. Temporary QA fixtures are excluded from publication.

A real iPhone/Safari, physical touch dragging, simultaneous human multiplayer clients and real-device FPS are not verified. Cloud-browser FPS is not a hardware benchmark. After an execution-environment restart, the source changes were reconstructed and regression/build checks repeated before publication.
